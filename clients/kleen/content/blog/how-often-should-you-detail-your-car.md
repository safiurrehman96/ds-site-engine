Most owners detail their car when it looks dirty. By then, the things a detail actually protects against — embedded grit, UV fade, salt corrosion — have already been at work for weeks. Here is the schedule we recommend to customers across Northern Virginia.

## The short answer

- **Daily drivers:** a full detail every 4–6 months, with a maintenance wash monthly.
- **Garage-kept weekend cars:** a full detail once or twice a year is usually enough.
- **Kids, pets, or commuting on Route 28:** interior details every 3 months pay for themselves.

## Why Virginia seasons matter

Winter is the hardest season on paint in the DMV. Road salt and brine sit in wheel wells and lower panels, and a January without a proper wash does more damage than a whole summer of pollen. We recommend a decontaminating wash in early spring specifically to reset after salt season.

Summer brings its own problem: tree sap and UV. If you park outdoors, a sealant or wax applied in late spring gives the paint a sacrificial layer through the hottest months.

## Signs you're overdue

If water no longer beads on the hood, the protective layer is gone. If the interior has a smell that airing out doesn't fix, it's in the fabric, not the air. Both are cheaper to address early.

A regular schedule keeps every visit shorter and cheaper than a rescue job. If you're not sure where your car falls, send us a photo and we'll tell you honestly.
