      Kleen Car Care was started by Hashim right here in Sterling, Virginia. Launched in 2025, this is a mobile detailing operation built on doing the work right on every single vehicle and letting the results speak for themselves. No shortcuts. No rushing. Just clean cars and satisfied customers.

      Hashim built this business without a franchise playbook or corporate backing. Just commitment to quality work, vehicle after vehicle, and a service model built around what customers actually need: a professional detail without disrupting their day.

  - heading: "Why Mobile Detailing"
    body: |
      The traditional model asks you to take time out of your day, drive to a shop, wait around or arrange a ride, and come back later to pick up your car. That is a hassle most people in Northern Virginia do not need. Kleen Car Care flipped that entirely. We load up our equipment, drive to your location, and handle everything on-site. You get your time back while your vehicle gets the attention it needs.

      This model works for everyone. Busy professionals commuting on Route 7 or the Dulles Toll Road. Parents who want the family SUV cleaned while the kids are at school. Anyone who values their time more than they value a trip to a shop.

      Note: We require access to your water supply and a standard power outlet at your location. Everything else comes with us.

  - heading: "What We Bring to Every Job"
    body: |
      Every detail starts with looking at your vehicle and understanding what it actually needs. A lightly maintained sedan does not get the same treatment as a truck that has seen three months of Northern Virginia commuting. We offer a full range of services from exterior wash to premium full detail, plus specialty detailing for motorcycles, ATVs, and golf carts.

      Add-ons including engine bay cleaning, headlight restoration, steam cleaning, pet hair removal, odor treatment, fabric UV protection, and black trim restoration are available on top of any service.
