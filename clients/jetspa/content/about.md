      JetSpa is a private aviation detailing company built for owners, flight departments, pilots, and FBOs who cannot afford anything less than exact work. Our team specializes in trip cleans, dry wash and decontamination, one and two step polish, ceramic coating up to five years, brightwork polishing, de-ice boot refurbishment, gear well cleaning, and full interior detailing. Every technician holds an Aircraft Detailing Specialist certification from the Aircraft Detailing Association (ADA), and every product we apply is approved by the original equipment manufacturers who built the aircraft we service.

      We built the business around a gap we saw in the Northeast market. Too many aircraft owners were forced to choose between overbooked competitors and unqualified subcontractors, and there was almost nothing in between. That gap is what JetSpa exists to close.

Between these four hangars, hundreds of aircraft movements happen every day, and our team can be on the ramp at any of them without waiting on gate clearance or third-party escorts.

  - heading: "Fully Insured, Fully Accountable"
    body: |
      You should never let an uninsured contractor near your aircraft. JetSpa carries $200,000 in general liability coverage and $1.5 million in hangar liability coverage, and we can provide certificates of insurance on request before any service. This is a standard we hold ourselves to as a matter of policy, and it is the same standard your insurance underwriter will expect of anyone touching your airframe.

If a product is not approved by the manufacturer of the aircraft we are working on, we do not use it. This applies to interior cleaners, exterior compounds, ceramic coatings, and brightwork polishes without exception.
