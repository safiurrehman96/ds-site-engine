intro:
  heading: "What Is Corporate Jet Detailing?"
  body: |
    Corporate jet detailing covers the full menu of appearance care for business jets: exterior wash, decontamination, polish and coating work, interior detail, brightwork polishing, boot refurbishment, and gear well cleaning. What changes between classes is the scale of the work.

    JetSpa services four broad classes of corporate jets:

    - **Light Corporate Jets.** Cirrus Vision SF50, HondaJet, Phenom 100/300, Citation JET series, Beechjet, Learjet 20/30/40 series, Hawker 400, Falcon 10.
    - **Midsize Corporate Jets.** Citation X, Citation Sovereign, Falcon 20/50/200, Praetor 500/600, PC-24, Learjet 45/55/60, Hawker 700/800, Astra.
    - **Large Corporate Jets.** Challenger 300/601/650, Global 350, Falcon 900/2000, Gulfstream GIII/G450, Embraer Legacy, RJ135, 650E.
    - **Super Corporate Jets.** Bombardier Global Express/5500/6500/7000, Dassault 8X/10X, Gulfstream G500/G600/G650/G650ER/G700, Embraer Lineage, Boeing Business Jet Max 7/8/9.

explainer:
  heading: "What Is Included"
  body: |
    Every service is scoped to the aircraft class. Typical corporate jet services include:

    - Exterior work: trip clean, dry wash, decontamination, one step polish, cut and polish, polymer sealant, ceramic coating (1, 2, or 5 year)
    - Interior work: trip clean, full interior detail, carpet extraction, lavatory and galley detail
    - Brightwork polishing on leading edges, exhaust stacks, engine inlets
    - De-ice boot refurbishment where applicable
    - Gear well cleaning

    Pricing is per aircraft class and service selection. A written scope with timeline is confirmed before the job begins. Larger aircraft require multi-day scheduling and larger crews.

whyItMatters:
  heading: "Why Corporate Jet Detailing Standards Matter"
  body: |
    **Class-appropriate chemistry.** Products approved for a Citation may not be approved for a Gulfstream. OEM approval is aircraft-specific, and we verify every product against the aircraft we are working on.

    **Team sizing.** A light jet can be detailed by a two-person crew in a day. A super-corporate jet cut and polish routinely requires six to eight people over five to eight days. Class dictates crew.

    **Downtime coordination.** Ceramic coating and boot refurbishment require 24 to 48 hours of ground time. On corporate schedules, that ground time has to be built into the maintenance window, not discovered after the fact.

    **Insurance alignment.** Higher-value aircraft demand higher-limit contractor insurance. Our $200,000 general liability and $1.5 million hangar liability coverage is documented and provided on request.

    **Documentation.** Corporate jets change hands. Service history documented in writing supports resale valuation and satisfies operator record requirements.
