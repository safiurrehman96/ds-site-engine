whyItMatters:
  heading: "Why Piston Aircraft Owners Should Use a Certified Detailer"
  body: |
    **Paint preservation.** Piston aircraft often sit outdoors, take heavier UV exposure than hangared jets, and accumulate exhaust residue faster than turbine aircraft. Regular professional detail preserves paint value.

    **Resale value.** The gap in resale value between a well-detailed and a neglected piston aircraft is often thousands of dollars. Documented service history matters at sale.

    **Oxidation reversal.** Once paint has oxidized, only proper machine polish will restore it. DIY hand polish rarely reverses years of neglect.

    **Interior condition.** Cabin odor removal and leather care require chemistry that homeowners do not have access to. Deep interior work is where DIY hits its limit.

    **Trust.** Certified, insured detailing on a piston aircraft is the same standard we apply to a Gulfstream. Aircraft class does not change the standard.

faqs:
  - q: "Do you service my hangared Cessna 172?"
    a: |
      Yes. All single engine piston aircraft in the general aviation fleet are within our service scope, including all Cessna 172 variants (Skyhawk, XP, RG).
  - q: "Can you coat a piston aircraft with ceramic?"
    a: |
      Yes. Ceramic coating is available for piston aircraft with 1 year, 2 year, and 5 year warranty options. Coating extends the effective life of the paint and is worth the investment for aircraft with several years of expected ownership.
  - q: "Is DIY really that different?"
    a: |
      For a regular wipe-down, no. For polish, coating, interior detail, or brightwork, yes. Machine polish requires products, pads, and technique that homeowners rarely have. Ceramic coating requires OEM-approved chemistry and controlled conditions.
  - q: "Can you polish the spinner on my piston?"
    a: |
      Yes. Spinner polish is available on any piston with a polished spinner, usually as an add-on to another service. Pricing per spinner is confirmed with your quote.
  - q: "How often should a piston aircraft be detailed?"
    a: |
      Depends on how the aircraft is stored and used. Hangared aircraft with regular use benefit from a full detail every 12 to 18 months plus quarterly trip cleans. Outdoor-based aircraft benefit from more frequent work to control oxidation.
