# The Team Behind Every JetSpa Detail

Every aircraft that comes through `{{custom_values.business_name}}` gets the same standard: certified hands, manufacturer-approved products, full insurance coverage, and a team that treats each airframe like the asset it is. No shortcuts, no substitutions, no learning on your aircraft.

## Who We Are

`{{custom_values.business_name}}` is a private aviation detailing company built for owners, flight departments, pilots, and FBOs who cannot afford anything less than exact work. Our team specializes in trip cleans, dry wash and decontamination, one and two step polish, ceramic coating up to five years, brightwork polishing, de-ice boot refurbishment, gear well cleaning, and full interior detailing. Every technician holds an Aircraft Detailing Specialist certification from the Aircraft Detailing Association (ADA), and every product we apply is approved by the original equipment manufacturers who built the aircraft we service.

We built the business around a gap we saw in the Northeast market. Too many aircraft owners were forced to choose between overbooked competitors and unqualified subcontractors, and there was almost nothing in between. That gap is what `{{custom_values.business_name}}` exists to close.

## Serving the Northeast's Busiest Corridors

`{{custom_values.business_name}}` holds badge access at four of the region's most active general aviation airports: Teterboro (KTEB), Morristown Municipal (KMMU), Lehigh Valley International in Allentown (KABE), and Westchester County / White Plains (KHPN). Between these four hangars, hundreds of aircraft movements happen every day, and our team can be on the ramp at any of them without waiting on gate clearance or third-party escorts.

For emergency work outside our primary catchment, we cover the greater Northeast including Baltimore, Connecticut, and the surrounding tri-state area. If your aircraft lands with an interior issue and the next departure is inside 24 hours, we can be there.

## Fully Insured, Fully Accountable

You should never let an uninsured contractor near your aircraft. `{{custom_values.business_name}}` carries $200,000 in general liability coverage and $1.5 million in hangar liability coverage, and we can provide certificates of insurance on request before any service. This is a standard we hold ourselves to as a matter of policy, and it is the same standard your insurance underwriter will expect of anyone touching your airframe.

Every technician on our team has passed the background screening required for airport badge access, holds current ADA certification, and follows OEM-approved chemistry protocols on every service we perform. If a product is not approved by the manufacturer of the aircraft we are working on, we do not use it. This applies to interior cleaners, exterior compounds, ceramic coatings, and brightwork polishes without exception.
