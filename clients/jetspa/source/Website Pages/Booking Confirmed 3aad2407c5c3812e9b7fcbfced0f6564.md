# APPOINTMENT CONFIRMED!

Your aircraft detail is booked with `{{custom_values.business_name}}`. Here is what happens next and how to prepare so we hit the ground running when we arrive.

## What Happens Next?

**Confirmation.** You will receive an email and text with your appointment details and a calendar invite. If you need to forward the details to your pilot, flight department, or FBO contact, everything you need is in that email.

**Pre-Service Coordination.** In the 24 hours before your appointment, our team will confirm hangar access, gate coordination with the FBO, and any specific ground handling requirements. If we need any information from your side (tail number, aircraft location, FBO of record), we will reach out.

**Service Day.** Our ADA-certified team arrives at your hangar or ramp position on schedule with all products, equipment, and PPE ready. We walk the aircraft with your pilot or ground crew, confirm scope, and begin work. Balance is due after satisfaction with the completed work.

## How to Prepare for Our Arrival

**Confirm FBO and Hangar Access.** Let your FBO know we are coming and confirm any escort or badge coordination is in place if needed. Our team holds badge access at KTEB, KMMU, KABE, and KHPN, but the FBO may still require your operator to add us as an approved vendor on their side.

**Ensure Aircraft Availability.** Make sure the aircraft is available for the full ground time window we quoted. Ceramic coating and de-ice boot refurbishment require 24 to 48 hours of no-fly time after service. If the aircraft is scheduled to depart before that window closes, contact us to reschedule.

**Clear the Cabin (Interior Services).** If interior detail or carpet extraction was booked, remove personal belongings, sensitive documents, and valuables from the cabin. Do not worry about dirt, stains, spills, or pet hair. That is exactly what we are here to handle.
