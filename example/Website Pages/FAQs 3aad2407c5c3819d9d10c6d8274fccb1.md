## Services and Aircraft

#### What aircraft do you service?

Every major aircraft class in general aviation: single and twin engine piston, single and twin engine turboprop, and every class of corporate jet from light through super-corporate and Boeing Business Jets. Helicopters are within scope on request.

#### Do you use FAA-approved products?

We use products approved by the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) of the aircraft we are servicing. OEM approval is the industry standard for aircraft appearance care chemistry. Product-specific approvals vary by airframe and coating brand and are verified in the quote process.

#### What is the difference between the 1 year, 2 year, and 5 year ceramic coating tiers?

The warranty period reflects the coating chemistry's expected durability under normal aircraft usage. Longer warranty coatings require more meticulous prep, more product, and more application time. Owners choosing between tiers should consider aircraft use rate, hangar conditions, and how long the aircraft will remain in current ownership. Learn more on the [aircraft ceramic coating](https://app.notion.com/aircraft-ceramic-coating) page.

#### Do you refurbish de-ice boots or just condition them?

Both. Refurbishment restores flexibility, appearance, and function to boots that are still structurally sound. Boots with active delamination, deep cracking, or tears require replacement, not refurbishment. Our assessment identifies which is which before work begins.

## Downtime, Cure, and Aftercare

#### How long is my aircraft grounded after ceramic coating?

24 to 48 hours minimum. During this window the aircraft cannot fly, be wet-washed, or exposed to precipitation. Flying earlier causes adhesion failure and voids the coating warranty. We tell you before service so scheduling is coordinated with your flight calendar.

#### How do I care for the coating between services?

Routine trip cleans and dry wash work with approved chemistry maintain the coating between annual services. Avoid harsh solvents, abrasive cleaning, and pressure washing on coated surfaces. We provide written aftercare guidelines with every coating service.

#### How often should I schedule a full detail?

Depends on aircraft class, hangar conditions, and use rate. Piston aircraft: 12 to 18 months for full detail plus quarterly trip cleans. Turboprops: annual full detail with boot refurbishment scheduled to your maintenance program. Jets: annual detail with coating cycles per warranty tier and interior work per use rate.
