## What Is Aircraft Interior Detailing?

Aircraft interior detailing is a specialized cabin restoration process covering carpet extraction, leather conditioning, wood veneer care, headliner cleaning, seat frame detail, and cabin surface disinfection. Unlike automotive interior work, aviation interiors must accommodate certified fabrics, flame-resistant materials, and finishes that are certified for the airframe. Using the wrong product can void material certifications or damage components that cost thousands to replace.

`{{custom_values.business_name}}` offers three service tiers:

- **Interior Trip Clean.** Fast turnaround refresh between charter legs. Vacuum, wipe-down, cabin surface disinfection, and light leather care. Typical time: 20 to 45 minutes.
- **Interior Detail.** Full cabin detail including leather conditioning, all surface cleaning, seat frame detail, headliner, and detailed vacuum. Typical time: 45 minutes to several hours depending on aircraft class.
- **Carpet Extraction.** Deep hot-water extraction for carpets and cabin fabrics. Bundled with interior detail for the fullest cabin refresh.

## Our Interior Detailing Process

Step one: **Walkthrough.** We inspect the cabin with your pilot or ground crew, identify problem areas such as stains, odors, or damage, and confirm scope in writing.

Step two: **Removal and vacuum.** All loose items are removed and the cabin receives a full detailed vacuum from headliner to floor.

Step three: **Surface work.** Every hard surface, leather element, and wood veneer is cleaned and conditioned with OEM-approved products.

Step four: **Deep work.** If carpet extraction was ordered, the extractor runs after surface work is complete to lift embedded soil and residue.

Step five: **Final pass.** We disinfect all high-touch surfaces (armrests, tray tables, cabin controls), replace loose items, and document the completed work.

## Why Aircraft Interior Detailing Matters

**Client experience.** Cabin condition is what your charter clients or passengers see and feel first. A dull cabin undercuts the premium the operator is charging.

**Material longevity.** Untreated leather cracks. Neglected wood dulls and micro-scratches. Contaminated carpets stain permanently. Regular interior detailing preserves the components you already paid for.

**Odor control.** Food spills and lavatory issues become permanent smells when not addressed early. Regular cleaning stops that cycle before it starts.

**Health.** Consistent disinfection of high-touch surfaces reduces microbial buildup, which matters especially for aircraft with frequent charter turnover.

**Resale value.** Cabin condition is one of the first things brokers photograph and appraisers note. Interior history affects aircraft valuation directly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### How long does an interior detail take?

Depends on aircraft class and scope. An Interior Trip Clean runs 20 to 45 minutes. A full Interior Detail on a light jet takes 45 to 90 minutes. Midsize and larger cabins take longer. Timeline is confirmed with your written quote.

#### Do you use OEM-approved products on cabin surfaces?

Yes. Every product touching a certified aviation surface is on the manufacturer approved list. This protects material certifications and prevents damage to leather, wood, and specialty fabrics.

#### Can you remove smoke or persistent food odors?

In most cases yes. Odor removal requires source elimination through deep extraction and fabric treatment, followed by ozone or hydrogen peroxide fogging. Persistent smoke odor from long-term interior smoking may require component-level intervention.

#### Do you clean the lavatory and galley?

Yes. Both are included in a full Interior Detail. Deeper lavatory cleans are available as an add-on for aircraft with heavier use.

#### Can this be done between charter flights?

Yes. Interior Trip Cleans are designed exactly for quick-turn refresh between legs. Book ahead through your scheduler, or same day when we have availability.
